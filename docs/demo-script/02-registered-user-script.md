# FanHub Plus — Registered member demonstration

Continue after the public visitor script. Allow approximately 4–5 minutes, including form interactions. Read only the **Narration** paragraphs aloud.

## 9. Registration and onboarding

**On screen:** Register a fresh demonstration account. Select three to five favourite fandoms and complete setup. Avoid displaying a password while typing.

**Narration:**

Registration adds a personal space to the public browsing experience. During onboarding, the user chooses three to five favourite fandoms. This gives the platform a clear starting point for understanding their interests.

The visual selection cards make this step feel like choosing the worlds they enjoy, rather than completing a long settings form. These preferences can be changed later as the user's interests develop.

## 10. First visit and personalised welcome

**On screen:** Pause on the new account's dashboard greeting. Show the selected fandom names, then revisit the dashboard to show the returning greeting.

**Narration:**

On the first dashboard visit, the user receives a welcome message that includes their favourite fandoms. On later visits, the greeting changes to welcome them back.

The idea is to make the dashboard feel like their own fan space from the beginning. If an account has no favourite fandoms saved, the dashboard uses a general welcome and invites the user to choose some, rather than showing an incomplete message.

## 11. How personalisation works

**On screen:** Show the saved fandoms and Recommended For You. Briefly return to a discovery page to explain preference ordering.

**Narration:**

Personalisation is based on the fandom categories the user has selected. In personalised listings, matching content receives priority, while content from other categories remains available.

This helps users find relevant stories, characters, events, and other content sooner without closing off new interests. Their searches and filters still narrow the results they want to explore.

For guests or users without saved preferences, the website falls back to its normal browsing order. This is preference-based personalisation: the user directly controls the interests behind it.

## 12. Dashboard overview

**On screen:** Show the overview cards, Continue Exploring, Favourite Fandoms, Recent Activity, Upcoming Releases, Recommended For You, and On Your Bookshelf. Use an established account for populated examples if needed.

**Narration:**

The overview brings the member's activity into one place. The summary cards show saved bookmarks, favourite fandoms, watched activity for the week, and upcoming releases.

Continue Exploring provides a route back to recently opened stories. Favourite Fandoms offers shortcuts into the user's interests, while Recent Activity records their interactions.

Recommendations and upcoming releases help them discover something new, and On Your Bookshelf brings saved items back into view. A new account sees helpful starting messages where there is no history yet.

The dashboard is designed around returning to content, discovering more, and managing personal choices.

## 13. Favourites and bookmarks

**On screen:** Open Fandoms and change a preference. Save a public item, then open Bookmarks and demonstrate the available note or removal controls.

**Narration:**

Favourite fandoms and bookmarks serve different purposes. Favourite fandoms describe the user's broader interests and influence personalisation. Bookmarks save individual items they want to revisit.

Members can manage their saved collection and add personal notes to bookmarks. This turns casual browsing into a collection they can organise over time.

## 14. Ratings and reviews

**On screen:** Open a supported detail page, submit a star rating and a short review, then show its status and the member Reviews page.

**Narration:**

Registered users can contribute through star ratings and written reviews on supported detail pages. The community area presents the available rating summary and published feedback.

A newly submitted review is visible to its author while awaiting approval. It becomes publicly visible after approval. The member's Reviews page provides a place to check and manage their own contributions.

This gives fans a voice while keeping a review process for public discussion.

## 15. Fan submissions and activity

**On screen:** Open My submissions and the creation form. Show the title, fandom, introduction, story, and cover fields. Then briefly open Activity.

**Narration:**

Members can also contribute their own stories through My submissions. The form connects a contribution to a fandom and collects its text and cover image. Members can track their submissions and the available status information from their account.

The Activity page brings their recorded interactions together, making it easier to understand and revisit their journey through the platform.

## 16. Settings, feedback, and closing

**On screen:** Show Settings without exposing personal details. Briefly open Feedback, then return to the dashboard or homepage.

**Narration:**

Settings lets members update their profile, display name, picture, and favourite fandoms, along with account controls such as changing their password. Feedback gives them a place to share suggestions about the platform.

FanHub Plus combines open discovery with a more personal experience after registration. Visitors can explore the content freely, while members can shape their recommendations, save discoveries, and contribute to the community.

The design goal is to bring many fandoms together while helping each person find a space that feels relevant to them. Thank you for watching this demonstration.
