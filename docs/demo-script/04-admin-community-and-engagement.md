# Admin demonstration — Community and engagement

Continue after file 03. Read only **Narration** aloud. Continue with file 05 afterward.

## 10. Submissions — review member contributions

**On screen:** Open Submissions, filter by status, and inspect a prepared member submission. Show approval and rejection actions. If approving a demo record, open the resulting public page.

**Narration:**

Submissions connects member contributions with the publishing workflow. The administrator can filter contributions by status and open a submission to inspect its author, content, category, and media.

Approval publishes the submission, while rejection keeps it from becoming public. This creates a review step between a member sending a story and that story appearing on the website. The system also attempts to notify the contributor of the decision by email.

## 11. Reviews — moderate written feedback

**On screen:** Open Reviews, inspect a pending review, and show approval/rejection controls. Use a prepared demo review for a public before-and-after example.

**Narration:**

Reviews handles written feedback about the site's supported items. The administrator can inspect the review and decide whether to approve or reject it, with a separate removal action available.

This explains the pending state shown to members after submission. Approved reviews can appear in the public community section, connecting moderation directly to what visitors see.

## 12. Ratings — inspect star scores

**On screen:** Open Ratings and show the available filters, score, user, and rated-item information.

**Narration:**

Ratings manages the numerical scores separately from written reviews. This page allows the administrator to inspect recorded ratings and remove an entry when necessary. Keeping ratings and reviews distinct makes it easier to understand both the score and the discussion around an item.

## 13. Feedback — track suggestions

**On screen:** Open Feedback, inspect a record, and show its status selector.

**Narration:**

Feedback collects suggestions and reports from members about the platform itself. The detail page presents the message, while the status control helps track its progress. This gives user suggestions a place in the maintenance and improvement process.

## 14. Users — inspect member accounts

**On screen:** Open Users, inspect an account's detail page, and briefly show the create form without submitting it.

**Narration:**

The Users module provides an overview of accounts and their available details. An administrator can inspect individual users and access the supported account-management actions.

The current creation form is specifically for creating an administrator account. Regular members join through the public registration flow. This distinction keeps the demonstration aligned with the actions this form actually supports.

## 15. Roles — maintain role records

**On screen:** Open Roles and inspect the create/edit forms. Keep existing account roles unchanged during recording.

**Narration:**

The Roles page manages role records and their descriptions. The main experiences demonstrated in this project are the administrator and the registered user. Administrator access is restricted to the administrative role, while members use the public account features.

This page provides a central place to inspect and maintain those role definitions.

## 16. Contacts — handle public enquiries

**On screen:** Open Contacts, inspect a message, and point to Reply via Email.

**Narration:**

Contacts contains messages submitted through the public contact form. The detail page brings together the sender, subject, message, and status. Reply via Email opens the mail application, allowing the administrator to respond to the sender.

This keeps general enquiries separate from member feedback and public reviews.

## 17. Subscribers — manage the newsletter audience

**On screen:** Open Subscribers, show filters and bulk controls, then inspect a subscriber's detail and edit pages. Point to export without exposing the downloaded data.

**Narration:**

Subscribers manages the audience that has signed up for email updates. The administrator can inspect a subscriber, update the available details and preferences, and manage subscription status.

The list also provides bulk actions and export controls for managing the collection efficiently. These subscription records provide the audience for the next module: newsletters.

## 18. Newsletters — prepare targeted updates

**On screen:** Open Newsletters, then create/edit, detail, and preview pages. Show subject, body, type, reference item, and recipient filters. Use a draft; do not send to real subscribers for the recording.

**Narration:**

Newsletters lets the administrator prepare updates for subscribers. A message includes its subject and body and can refer to an item such as content, an event, a character, merchandise, or a category.

Recipient filters allow the audience to be narrowed using the available controls. Previewing the message before sending helps check its presentation. The detail page provides the available status and sending information, completing the flow from preparing content to communicating it to subscribers.

## 19. Chatbot FAQs — prepare maintained answers

**On screen:** Open Chatbot FAQs and show the list, create/edit form, and a saved FAQ's detail page.

**Narration:**

Chatbot FAQs provides a place to maintain common questions and answers, with an optional category. An exact question match can return a maintained answer directly. FAQs also support suggested questions and provide reference context for the AI fallback.

This gives the administrator a practical way to improve answers to recurring questions through content they can inspect and edit.

## 20. Chatbot — understand visitor questions

**On screen:** Open Chatbot Queries. Show search/filter controls, recorded responses, and export controls using demonstration data.

**Narration:**

The Chatbot page records questions and responses so the administrator can inspect how the assistant is being used. The filters distinguish FAQ matches from AI responses.

Reviewing these questions can reveal topics that need clearer explanations or additional FAQs. This connects visitor questions with ongoing improvements to the support content.

## 21. Analytics — review the wider picture

**On screen:** Open Analytics. Show views and user growth, category/status charts, popular content and merchandise, review moderation, feedback, and rating distribution.

**Narration:**

Analytics provides a broader view of the platform's recorded activity. It brings together views, user growth, content distribution, popular items, and community measures such as ratings and review status.

The main dashboard helps the administrator decide what needs attention today. Analytics supports a deeper look at patterns across the platform, helping guide future publishing and community decisions.
