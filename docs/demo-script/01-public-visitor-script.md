# FanHub Plus — Public visitor demonstration

Read the **Narration** paragraphs aloud. **On screen** lines are recording directions, not spoken text. Continue with `02-registered-user-script.md` after this file. Allow approximately 5 minutes, including navigation pauses.

## 1. Introduction and homepage

**On screen:** Open the homepage while signed out. Pause on the hero, then show the navigation and category cards.

**Narration:**

Hello, and welcome to FanHub Plus. This project brings different fandoms into one place, where visitors can discover stories, characters, videos, events, upcoming releases, and merchandise. I will first demonstrate the website as a visitor, then show how the experience changes for a registered member.

The homepage introduces the platform through a cinematic video hero. The idea behind this design is to make the website feel connected to the entertainment worlds that fans enjoy. The text and navigation give visitors a clear starting point, while the category cards provide quick routes into their interests.

## 2. Homepage discovery sections

**On screen:** Scroll through fandoms, Trending Now, featured stories, fan content, Meet the Icons, upcoming releases, events, and merchandise. Open one trending story to demonstrate navigation, then return.

**Narration:**

The homepage works as a guided introduction to the whole platform. Fandom categories help visitors choose a world to explore, while Trending Now and featured stories highlight content worth discovering.

The fan-content section gives the community a place in that experience. Meet the Icons introduces characters through visual cards, followed by upcoming releases, events, and merchandise.

The design uses strong images, clear headings, and short previews so visitors can scan the page without reading everything. Each section leads into a more focused page, keeping the homepage useful without placing every detail in one long list.

## 3. Fandoms, discovery, and content details

**On screen:** Open a fandom, demonstrate an available search or filter, and open an article. Briefly show a video or audio item if available.

**Narration:**

The fandom and discovery pages bring related content together. Visitors can narrow the available results using the page's search and filter controls, then open an item for the full experience.

On a detail page, the title, artwork, and content have more room. Articles provide a reading experience, while media content provides its relevant player when available. This separates quick discovery from focused reading or viewing.

Visitors can browse public content without creating an account. Signing in adds personal actions, such as saving items and participating in ratings and reviews.

## 4. Characters

**On screen:** Open Characters, select a character, and show its description and community section.

**Narration:**

The Characters section helps fans discover the personalities behind their favourite worlds. A character card leads to a dedicated page with artwork and the available background information.

The layout keeps the character image prominent while giving the written information space to remain readable. The community section also lets visitors see the ratings and published reviews available for that character.

## 5. Events

**On screen:** Show the spotlight, then the event filters. Apply one filter, open a result, and show the event facts. Show nearby discovery if location access is working.

**Narration:**

The Events page connects online interests with real-world activities. The spotlight introduces featured events, and the main listing helps visitors search by details such as fandom, city, event type, or date.

An event's detail page brings together its description, date, time, location, and status. Where provided, visitors can open the venue map or follow the official event or ticket link.

Nearby discovery offers another way to find events using location information. The design moves from an engaging introduction to practical details, helping someone decide whether an event interests them.

## 6. Upcoming releases

**On screen:** Open Upcoming, demonstrate a filter, and open a release.

**Narration:**

Upcoming Releases gives fans a place to see what is coming next. Visitors can browse the available releases, use the page controls to narrow their choices, and open an item for more information.

Release dates help users plan ahead. When a date has not been announced, the interface communicates that instead of presenting an invented date.

## 7. Merchandise

**On screen:** Open Merchandise. Show search, category, release-status and edition filters, then open an item.

**Narration:**

The Merchandise section is a discovery catalogue for fandom-related items. Visitors can search the collection and filter by category, release status, or edition, with sorting options to make browsing easier.

The detail page shows the item's artwork, description, release information, and linked story or character when available. Members can save an item to their bookmarks to return to it later.

Consistent image containers keep different product photographs balanced across the grid, while related items encourage further discovery.

## 8. Support, themes, and navigation

**On screen:** Briefly open Contact, the chatbot, and Sitemap. Show the footer, switch theme, then demonstrate a narrow mobile viewport.

**Narration:**

The Contact page provides a direct way to send an enquiry, and the floating chatbot offers another place to ask questions. The footer brings important navigation and newsletter subscription together, while the sitemap provides an organised overview of the website.

Light and dark themes give visitors a choice of viewing style. The layouts also adapt to smaller screens, so navigation, cards, and forms remain usable on mobile. Throughout the website, the shared colours, typography, and branding keep the different sections connected.
